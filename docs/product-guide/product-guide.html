01

MultiSims USA in one page

MultiSims USA sells US phone lines to people who need one: travellers, students and anyone moving to the US. A line is a plan from a US carrier, sold in day packages, and it comes as an eSIM or as a physical SIM card. Customers pay in crypto. Your team fills every order by hand from a shared queue.

Four kinds of people use it

Customer
multisimsusa.com, no account

Picks a plan, pays in crypto and gets the line on a private order page and by email. Can refill the number later, activate a SIM card, rate the order and report a problem.

Agent
Agent portal, signs in

Takes paid orders from the queue and fills them on the carrier's side: QR codes, phone numbers, ICCIDs, tracking, refills. Answers support tickets.

Dealer
Dealer portal, signs in

A shop or reseller that buys in bulk at its own prices. Pays from a prepaid wallet, orders many SIMs at once and refills its customers' numbers.

Owner
Admin portal, signs in

You. Sets carriers, plans and prices, manages agents and dealers, adds dealer funds, decides on problems and follows the numbers.

The plan timer

Every active line knows when its plan ends. Customers, dealers, agents and you all see it in the same words, for example Active until Nov 27, 2026, 30 days left. When a refill is bought while a plan is still running, the new days start the day after it ends, and the line shows Next plan starts Nov 28, 2026, until Dec 27, 2026. Plan days are counted in US Eastern time.

03 · Role

Customers

Customers never make an account. They buy on the website, pay in crypto and keep a private link to their order. Most of them arrive on a phone, so every screen is made for a phone first.

What a customer can do

  • Browse and compare plans
  • Buy an eSIM or a SIM card with a plan
  • Buy a SIM card on its own
  • Add international calling and use a discount code
  • Follow the order on its private page
  • Refill a number and activate a SIM card
  • Rate the order and each SIM
  • Report a problem and get an answer

3.1Finding a plan

  1. The home page and Plans list every plan. They can be filtered by eSIM or SIM card and by carrier, and sorted by price or data. The search at the top finds a plan by carrier or name.
  2. A plan page shows the data, hotspot, talk and text, and the countries covered. The customer picks how many days they need, and can switch on international calling and see where they can call.
  3. Under the price the page says what checkout adds, for example the SIM card and shipping, so the total is never a surprise.

3.2Buying an eSIM

  1. On Buy now the customer comes to checkout with the plan and days already chosen. They can change the number of eSIMs.
  2. They enter their name, email, phone or WhatsApp and the US ZIP code where they will use the line. An area code is optional.
  3. A discount code goes under Have a discount code?.
  4. If the order is under the minimum, the button stays off and says why, next to the button.
  5. They pay in Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT or USDC on the payment page, then come back to their order page. The order page link is also emailed.

3.3Buying a SIM card with a plan

The same checkout, with a US delivery address and one choice more: start the plan when the SIM arrives, or on a date at least three days away. Shipping is charged once per order, however many cards.

  1. The agent posts the card. The customer gets the USPS tracking number by email and on the order page.
  2. When the card arrives, the customer presses My SIM has arrived on the order page. If they chose a date, nothing is needed: the plan starts that day.
  3. The agent activates the line and the customer gets the phone number.

3.4Buying a SIM card on its own

Under SIM cards on their own on the Plans page, each carrier's card is listed with its price and no length. At checkout the plan is set to No plan, SIM card only, and no ZIP code is asked. The card is posted with tracking. When the customer is ready to use it, they press Activate this SIM on the order page.

3.5The order page

Each order has its own private page. It is the only way back to the order, so the link is emailed and the page tells the customer to bookmark it. The page updates on its own and shows:

  • The status as Placed, Processing, Shipped and Delivered, with dates.
  • For an eSIM, the QR code with installation steps for iPhone and Android, the phone number and ICCID with copy buttons.
  • For a SIM card, the tracking number with a Track button, and then the phone number.
  • The plan timer on every active line, and the order details with every price.

3.6Refilling a number

A refill adds days to a number without changing the SIM. There are two ways in:

  • Refill this number on a delivered line. The form opens already filled in.
  • Refill in the menu, at multisimsusa.com/refill. This also works for a number bought somewhere else: the customer picks the carrier and types the number, and the ICCID if they have it.

They choose the plan and the days and pay. If the current plan is still running, the new days start the day after it ends, so no day is lost. Only plans you offer for refills appear here.

3.7Activating a SIM card

On the same page, Activate a new SIM card starts a plan on a card bought on its own. The customer gives the ICCID printed on the card, the ZIP code and an optional area code, and picks a SIM card plan. The agent activates it and the customer gets the number.

3.8Ratings and problems

  • Rating. Once the line works, the customer can rate the order and each SIM from 1 to 5 stars with a comment, and change it later. Nothing is shown on the website until you feature it.
  • Report a problem. On a delivered order the customer picks what is wrong, how urgent it is, describes it and can add a screenshot. Your team answers on the order page and by email.
  • Taking longer than usual. If an order waits a while, a Contact us box appears on the order page so the customer can reach you.

3.9Emails a customer gets

Order received, payment confirmed, SIM card shipped with tracking, line delivered with the QR code or number and the plan dates, and every answer to a problem they reported.

04 · Role

Agents

Agents fill the orders. They work from one shared queue, take an order, get the line from the carrier and deliver it. While an order is theirs, nobody else can touch it.

An agent is responsible for

  • Claiming paid orders quickly, oldest first, and filling them correctly.
  • Entering exactly what the carrier gives: QR code, phone number, ICCID, tracking number, plan dates.
  • Flagging an order to you when it cannot be filled as ordered, instead of guessing.
  • Answering support tickets and issuing replacements when a SIM does not work.

4.1The queue

Agents sign in at multisimsusa.com/portal/login and land on the queue. At the top they see how many orders are waiting, how many are theirs, the oldest wait and what is waiting for you. Below that:

  • Yours: orders this agent claimed and has not finished.
  • Waiting: paid orders nobody has taken, oldest first, each with a Claim button. Tags show Refill, Activate SIM or SIM cards only.
  • Needs the owner: orders an agent flagged, waiting for your decision.
  • Scheduled activations: SIM cards that start on a chosen date.

New orders appear on their own with a sound (switched on from the bar at the top) and a notification. Every agent also gets an email for each new order.

4.2Claiming and filling an order

  1. Press Claim. The order opens and is now yours. If another agent was faster, the page says so and shows who has it.
  2. The panel on the right, Line to get, says exactly what to order from the carrier: carrier and plan, days, quantity, ZIP code and area code, and international calling if bought.
  3. Fill each SIM: drop or paste the QR image, type the phone number and ICCID, and press Save. For many SIMs, the bulk tools take a column of numbers and ICCIDs pasted from a spreadsheet, and several QR images at once.
  4. When every SIM is ready, press the main button and confirm. The customer is told straight away.

4.4Refill dates

On a refill the agent sees when the number's current plan ends, if we sold or refilled it before, and the proposed dates for the new days: the day after the current plan ends if it is still running, otherwise the day it is completed. If the number is not in our records, the page says so and the agent checks it in the carrier's portal. The dates can be changed before delivering. A date in the past is refused, and an overlap with the current plan shows a warning.

4.5When something is wrong

  • Needs attention sends the order to you with a reason, for example "Area code 212 is not available today". It waits under Needs the owner until you decide.
  • Release puts the order back in the queue for someone else.
  • Tickets lists problems reported by customers and dealers, the most urgent first. The agent writes one clear answer and sets the status. The customer sees it on the order page and by email.
  • Issue replacement creates a free new order for a SIM that does not work. It goes straight to the queue, or waits for your approval if you chose that in Settings.
05 · Role

Dealers

Dealers are shops and resellers that buy in bulk at their own prices. They never pay at checkout. They keep a balance with you, and each order is paid from it.

A dealer is responsible for

  • Keeping enough money in the wallet for the orders they want to place.
  • Ordering the right plan, length and quantity, with a US address for SIM cards.
  • Sending correct numbers and ICCIDs for refills.
  • Telling you about a problem through a ticket, with the SIM it concerns.

5.1The wallet

The wallet shows four figures, each with a short explanation on screen:

FigureMeaning
Available to spendBalance minus what is held, plus any credit limit.
BalanceAll the money in the wallet.
Held for ordersReserved for orders and refills not done yet. Taken as each SIM is delivered, shipped or refilled, and returned if cancelled.
Credit limitHow far the dealer may spend beyond the balance. Zero unless you agree otherwise.

Money comes in two ways: a crypto top up from the Wallet page, or funds you add by hand after a bank transfer or a payment on WhatsApp. Every movement is listed in the history with its note, and none can be edited or deleted.

5.2Placing an order

  1. Open New order and add lines. Each line is one plan and length with its own quantity, up to 500 SIMs a line and 1,000 an order. Prices are the dealer's own.
  2. SIM cards on their own are at the top of the plan list. A dealer with no SIM card price yet is told to contact you.
  3. International calling can be added per line, and a ZIP code and area code for where the numbers should be from (both optional). A reference, such as a shop or purchase order, helps the dealer find the order later.
  4. Device details (optional): one SIM per row, for example the IMEI and EID, pasted straight from a list. They can also be added or fixed on the order page, with photos, until the SIMs are delivered.
  5. For SIM cards, a US delivery address. All cards in the order ship together with one tracking number.
  6. The summary shows what is available, the order total and what is left after. If the money is not enough, it says how much more is needed.

5.3Following an order

Orders lists every bulk order with its status and progress, for example 7 of 10 SIMs live. Inside an order the dealer sees each SIM with its phone number, ICCID, QR code link and plan timer, how much was taken and how much is still held. Download CSV exports every SIM for the dealer's own system. Activations lists every line the dealer has received, searchable by phone number, ICCID or order number, and also exports to CSV.

5.4Refilling numbers

  1. Open Refill and choose the plan and length for a line.
  2. Paste the numbers, one per row, with the ICCID after a comma if known. Numbers can be written in any common way.
  3. Each wrong row is named: not a US number, an ICCID of the wrong length, or a number already in this refill. A number is never charged twice.
  4. Place the refill. The money is held and taken as each number is refilled. Numbers still running start their new days the day after the current plan ends.

The Refill section only lists plans you offer for refills and that have a price for this dealer. Otherwise the page says refills are not open yet, or that this dealer has no price for those plans.

5.5Support and ratings

From an order the dealer can report a problem on a line or a single SIM, and rate a line or a SIM. Dealer ratings are for you only and never appear on the website. Under Login the dealer can change their password.

06 · Role

You, the owner

You run the business from the admin portal. You decide what is sold and at what price, who works and who buys in bulk, and you step in when something needs a decision. The separate Admin guide goes through every setting in detail.

The owner is responsible for

  • Carriers, plans, day packages and prices
  • SIM card prices and discount codes
  • Which plans can be refilled
  • Agents and their logins
  • Dealers, their prices and their funds
  • Decisions on flagged orders and refunds
  • Which reviews appear on the website
  • Fees, the minimum order and alerts

6.1Your day at a glance

Overview shows sales, orders paid, SIMs delivered, orders waiting now and dealer funds received, each compared with the period before. Needs attention appears only when something needs you, such as a flagged order. The bell lists new orders, flagged orders, tickets and dealer top ups as they happen, and you get an email when an order waits too long.

6.2Your catalogue

  • Carriers: add a network with its logo and show or hide it. Hiding a carrier hides all its plans at once.
  • SIM cards: one row per carrier with the website price, the dealer price and whether the card is sold on its own.
  • Plans: the plan details, then day packages, each with a price, an optional sale price and a wholesale price for dealers. A plan is on the website when it is published, its carrier is shown and at least one package is on sale.
  • Offer for refills: switch it on for every plan customers and dealers may refill with.
  • Plan type: Daily or Monthly. It decides which phone details buyers are asked for: an eSIM the IMEI and EID; a SIM card the IMEI and ICCID (daily) or the EID and ICCID (monthly). All optional.
  • Add-ons sets international calling, and Discounts creates codes by percentage or amount, for one carrier or all, with dates and a use limit.

Every change shows on the website within seconds. There is nothing to publish separately.

6.3Orders and support

Orders lists every order with filters for status, product, agent, website or dealer, and a search by order number, name, email, phone number or ICCID. On an order you can fill it yourself, reassign it, send a flagged order back with an instruction, cancel it with a reason only your team sees, mark it paid or record a refund. Tickets, Activations and Customers sit next to it. Customers can be exported for your records.

6.4Agents

Create an agent with a name and email. A password is suggested and shown once with copy buttons, so you can send the email and password together. You can reset a password or switch a login off when someone leaves; their claimed orders go back to the queue.

6.5Dealers and their money

  1. Create the dealer with company, contact name and email, and send them the login.
  2. Set their prices. Leave a box empty to use your normal wholesale price. SIM card prices can be set per carrier.
  3. Set a credit limit if you allow one. Zero means they pay first.
  4. Add funds when they pay you. To fix a mistake use Correction, add or take, with a note the dealer sees. A correction can never take more than the money in the wallet.

Suspending a dealer stops new orders and sign ins. Orders already in progress are still filled.

6.6Reviews, reports and settings

  • Reviews: nothing appears on the website until you switch on Featured. A review that is changed later comes off until you feature it again.
  • Reports: sales, deliveries, tickets and replacements for any dates, by carrier, dealer, agent or channel, each table with a CSV download.
  • Settings: your alert email and waiting time, the minimum order, shipping, the network fee (leave it at 0), how replacements work and the problem types customers can choose.

08

Good to know

  • Payments are in crypto, Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT on TRC-20 or USDC on Ethereum (ERC-20), through NOWPayments. An order only reaches the queue once the payment is confirmed. Every pay button asks for the exact amount the payment page shows. If a customer still pays too little, nothing is filled, you are told, and the order shows what reached your wallet so you can accept it or refund it. A dealer who tops up too little gets what reached your wallet, by itself.
  • The minimum order is checked before payment, after any discount. Discount codes never reduce shipping.
  • Crypto network fee. Our payment partner adds the network fee for the coin the customer chooses on its own payment page, so the crypto amount there is a little higher than the order total. Your payout is the order total less the partner's own service fee.
  • Times and plan days are shown in US Eastern time everywhere, in one date style.
  • Privacy: an order page only shows that customer's own order. A refill never shows anything about a number until the refill is done.
  • Wording: customers and dealers see Processing, Shipped and Delivered. The site never says a person fills the order.
  • Phones and dark mode: every page, including the portals, works on a phone and follows the light or dark setting of the device.
