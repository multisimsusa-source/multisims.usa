1Signing in and finding your way

Go to multisimsusa.com/portal/login and sign in with your email and password. You land on the Overview. Agents and dealers use the same sign in page and each of them only sees their own portal.

The menu on the left is grouped the way you work:

GroupWhat is there
TopOverview and Reports
OrdersOrders, Agent queue, Tickets, Activations, Customers, Reviews
CatalogueCarriers, Plans, Add-ons, Discounts
TeamAgents, Dealers, Settings

The bar at the top shows the bell (your notifications), a sound switch for new order alerts, a link to the website and the light or dark theme. Your name at the bottom of the menu opens your account and Sign out.

Everything works on a phone. On a small screen the menu opens from the button at the top left.

2Your day at a glance

Overview

The tiles at the top show sales, orders paid, SIMs delivered, orders waiting right now and dealer funds that came in. Under each figure you see how it compares with the period before. Change the period with the box above the tiles (today, 7 days, 30 days, this month or your own dates).

Needs attention appears only when something needs you, for example an order an agent flagged or a replacement waiting for your approval. Click it and deal with it first.

Notifications

The bell shows new orders, flagged orders, customer messages, new tickets and dealer top ups as they happen. A number on the bell means unread items. Click one to open it, or use Mark all as read.

Emails

You also get an email when an order sits unclaimed for too long, when a customer sends a message and when a ticket comes in. The address and the waiting time are set in Settings.

3Carriers and plans

Carriers

In Carriers you add a network with its name and logo, and switch On website on or off. Hiding a carrier hides all of its plans at once, which is handy when a network has a problem. Carriers show on the home page in the order of this list.

Adding a plan

  1. Open Plans and click New plan.
  2. Pick the carrier and the product: eSIM or SIM card.
  3. Fill in the plan details: name, a short summary, data allowance, hotspot, talk, text, network and speed, any slowdown after a data limit and the countries covered. Leave a field empty if it does not apply.
  4. Save. The plan is created as a draft, so nobody sees it yet.
  5. Add the day packages (see the next part). Quick fill days adds the usual lengths in one click.
  6. Switch Published on and save.
When is a plan on the website? Only when three things are true: the plan is published, its carrier is on the website, and at least one day package is on sale. The plan page tells you which one is missing.

Day packages

Each plan is sold in lengths of days, and each length has its own price. A package row has:

FieldMeaning
LengthNumber of days, any number you like
PriceThe normal price on the website
SaleOptional. A lower price for a promotion. Leave empty for no sale
WholesaleWhat dealers pay. Leave empty and dealers will not see this length
On saleSwitch off to stop selling one length without deleting it

Price changes show on the website within a few seconds.

Pausing or removing a plan

To stop selling a plan for a while, switch Published off and save. It keeps everything and you can put it back any time. Delete plan removes it for good and its page stops working, so only use it for plans you will never sell again.

4Prices, sales and discount codes

Sale prices

Put a price in the Sale field of a package. The website then shows the sale price with the normal price crossed out and a Sale label. Clear the field to end the sale.

Please note. Only show a crossed out price that you really charged before. US rules do not allow made up "was" prices.

Discount codes

In Discounts click New discount code and set:

  • the code customers type, for example SUMMER10
  • a percentage or a fixed amount off
  • optionally one carrier only, start and end dates, and how many times it can be used

Customers enter it at checkout under "Have a discount code?". Codes work on website orders only. They never reduce shipping or the network fee, and the minimum order is checked after the discount. A use is only counted when the order is paid. Switch a code off at any time to stop it.

5International calling

In Add-ons you set the price of international calling (website and wholesale) and whether it is offered. Customers switch it on when they pick their days.

The list of countries and their minute caps comes from the carrier's rate table. Customers see it under "See where you can call" on the plan page and at checkout. When the carrier sends a new table, use Replace list on the same page.

6Orders

Agents fill orders from the agent queue. As the owner you mostly watch, and step in when something goes wrong.

What the statuses mean

StatusMeaning
Awaiting paymentCustomer started checkout but has not paid yet
In queuePaid and waiting for an agent
ClaimedAn agent is working on it
Needs attentionAn agent flagged it for you
ShippedA SIM card was posted, with a tracking number
DeliveredThe eSIM or the SIM details were sent to the customer
CancelledStopped, nothing more happens

Customers and dealers only ever see Processing, Shipped, Delivered or Cancelled.

What you can do on an order

Open any order from Orders. Depending on its status you can:

  • Reassign it to another agent, or put it back in the queue
  • Fill it yourself, the same way an agent does
  • Send back with an instruction when an agent flagged it, for example "Deliver the nearest available area code"
  • Mark as paid if a payment arrived but the order did not update
  • Cancel order, with a short reason only you see
  • Record a refund, with the details of the crypto you sent back

Use the search and filters at the top of the list to find an order by number, name, email or phone.

Activations and customers

Activations lists every SIM that was delivered or shipped. Search it by phone number, ICCID, order number or email. Customers lists everyone who ordered on the website and can be downloaded as a file for your records.

7Tickets and replacements

Customers report a problem from their order page. Dealers do the same from their portal. Each report becomes a ticket in Tickets, the most urgent first.

Open a ticket, write one clear answer and set its status. The customer sees the answer on their order page and gets it by email.

If a SIM has to be replaced, use Issue replacement. It creates a free new order that goes to the queue. If you switched on "Replacements wait for my approval" in Settings, replacements raised by agents wait for you first.

You choose which problem types customers can pick (for example No signal) in Settings.

8Agents

  • New agent: in Agents, enter their name and email. A password is suggested for you. After you save, the window shows the email and password with copy buttons: send exactly those to the agent. The password is not shown again.
  • Reset password: set a new one from the agent's row and copy it from the window the same way. The old password stops working at once.
  • Switch off a login when someone leaves. They can no longer sign in, and any orders they had claimed go back to the queue.

Every active agent gets the new order and ticket emails.

9Dealers and their balance

Dealers buy in bulk from their own portal. They pay from a balance held with you, never at checkout.

Setting up a dealer

  1. In Dealers click New dealer. Enter the company, the contact's name and email. After you save, copy the email and password from the window and send them to the dealer; the welcome email tells them to use the password you gave them. If a dealer cannot sign in, set a new password from their page and send it again. A dealer you switched off is told the account is switched off.
  2. Open the dealer. Under the prices grid, set any prices that differ from your normal wholesale prices. Leave a box empty to use the normal one. Click Save prices.
  3. Set the Dealer SIM card price if this dealer pays a different amount per physical SIM card.
  4. Set Credit allowed. This is how far below zero they may order. 0 means they pay first.
  5. Add funds so they can start ordering.

Adding money

There are two ways money comes in:

  • They top up in crypto from their Wallet page. The money is added by itself once the payment is complete. If they pay less than the invoice, nothing is added and you get an email, so you can add the right amount by hand.
  • You add funds by hand, for example after a bank transfer or a payment on WhatsApp. On the dealer page use Add funds, enter the amount and a note such as "Paid on WhatsApp for 100 activations".

To fix a mistake use Correction, choose Add to balance or Take from balance and say what was wrong. The dealer sees the note next to the amount. Nothing in the wallet history can be edited or deleted, so every change stays on record.

How their balance moves

When a dealer places an order, the full amount is held. Each SIM is only charged when it is delivered. If you cancel an order, whatever was not delivered goes back to their balance. A refund on a dealer order is paid back into their balance.

Suspending a dealer

Switch the dealer to Suspended to stop new orders and sign ins. Orders already in progress are still filled.

10Reviews

When an order is delivered, the customer can rate it from their order page or from the delivery email. A SIM card order can be rated as soon as the card arrives (the customer presses "My SIM has arrived", or the start date they chose comes). With more than one SIM, the customer can also rate each SIM on its own, and replacements too. Dealers rate their lines and SIMs from their portal. All ratings arrive in Reviews, saying what was rated (Whole order, eSIM 2, Replacement eSIM); the From filter separates customers and dealers. An order's ratings also show on its order screen.

Customers and dealers can change a rating later. A changed review is marked "changed" and comes off the website until you switch Featured on again, so you always see the new words first. Dealer ratings are never shown on the website.

Nothing shows on the website until you switch Featured on for a review. Featured reviews appear on the home page with the customer's first name, stars and comment. The site never shows an average or a count. Use Hide for reviews you do not want to keep in the list.

11Reports

Reports shows sales, deliveries, tickets and replacements for any dates. Narrow it by carrier, dealer, agent or channel (website or dealer). Each table has a total row and a CSV button that downloads it for Excel or Google Sheets.

13Good to know

  • Changes to carriers, plans and prices show on the website straight away. There is nothing to publish separately.
  • The website never says a person fills the orders. Customers see Processing, Shipped and Delivered.
  • Payments are in crypto through NOWPayments. An order only moves to the queue once the payment is confirmed.
  • Customers do not need an account. Each order has its own private link, sent by email, where they see their eSIM, tracking and messages.
  • If something looks wrong, take a screenshot and send it to us on WhatsApp with the order number.
